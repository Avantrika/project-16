Transparent Risk Engine Weights

Every simulated order is evaluated across 6 normalized factors (0–100) before computing the composite risk score:

Risk Dimension Weight Core Detection Logic
Loss Chasing 30% Trade placed shortly after loss with increased position size
Overtrading 20% Frequency bursts (>4 trades in 15 min or >daily limit)
FOMO / Hype 20% Self-reported influencer, social media, or sudden spike influence
Risk Escalation 15% Steadily increasing order sizes across consecutive executions
Personal Rules 10% Safe budget breach, max loss limit breach, or off-hours
Emotional Signal 5% Self-reported tilt states (Angry, Frustrated, Anxious)
Composite = (LossChasing×0.30) + (Overtrading×0.20) + (FOMO×0.20) + (Escalation×0.15) + (Rule×0.10) + (Emotion×0.05)
Concrete Cooling-Off Triggers (Section 10)

Automatic algorithmic interventions that lock trading execution into strict countdowns:

Trigger 1: Loss Chasing Streak 30-Min Lock
3 losing trades within 30 minutes + next trade amount is larger.
Trigger 2: Extreme Overtrading Burst 15-Min Lock
5 or more simulated trades placed within a 15-minute rolling window.
Trigger 3: Financial Capital Safety Override 30-Min Lock
Emergency/essential funds = YES or Borrowed money = YES (Overrides score).
Trigger 4: Compound Risk Convergence 30-Min Lock
Multiple high-risk signals (Score ≥ 60/100) occurring simultaneously.
Trigger 5: Midnight Impulse Window 30-Min Lock
Trading after midnight (00:00 - 05:00) coupled with elevated emotion/size.
Action Tiers & Intervention Protocols
Score 0 – 29
ALLOW SIMULATED TRADE

No major behavioural risk pattern detected. Trade executed smoothly and logged into Decision Journal.

Score 30 – 59
WARNING + REFLECTION

Highlights detected cognitive patterns. Asks trader to review decision parameters before continuing.

Score 60 – 100
MANDATORY COOLING-OFF

Enforces real JavaScript countdown lock (15–30 min). Trader must pause, review guided prompts, and reconsider.
