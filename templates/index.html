Behavioural Harm Interception

Detects harmful human biases: Loss Chasing (escalating trade size after a loss), Overtrading (excessive intraday bursts), FOMO/Hype (chasing influencer and social calls), and Risk Escalation.

Financial Safety Override

Non-negotiable protection: If capital is indicated as emergency/essential money or borrowed funds, the system triggers an immediate HIGH-RISK Override and mandatory 30-minute cooling-off, regardless of other metrics.

Concrete Cooling-Off Timers

Enforces strict JavaScript countdown locks (15 to 30 minutes) based on concrete trigger rules (e.g. 3 losing trades in 30 minutes, or 5 trades in 15 minutes) to break emotional dopamine loops.
